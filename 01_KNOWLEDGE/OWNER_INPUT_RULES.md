# OWNER INPUT RULES

Owner knowledge is a first-class source.

Voice answers, interviews and personal experience should be preserved in raw form before editing.

Processing:
RAW → CLEANED → STRUCTURED → KNOWLEDGE.

Never replace raw owner material with an AI rewrite.

If an owner statement conflicts with an external source:
- preserve the owner statement;
- preserve the external source;
- mark the conflict;
- do not silently choose one.
