# CONTENT WORK RULES

Every content item needs:
- audience;
- topic;
- objective;
- format/channel;
- source context;
- factual basis;
- CTA if appropriate.

Writing must sound human and match the owner's voice profile.

Do not invent:
- achievements;
- prices;
- schedules;
- events;
- student results;
- staff;
- testimonials.

Content should be useful even when the reader does not buy immediately.
