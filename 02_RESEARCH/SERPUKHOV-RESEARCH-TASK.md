# SERPUKHOV PILOT — INITIAL ANALYST TASK

## Objective
Create the first complete analytical baseline for a children's dance school operating in Serpukhov.

## Business context
The school has a long-standing expert base in dance education and sports dance. The future content system will combine:
- owner's expert knowledge;
- continuously collected external research;
- audience understanding;
- local SEO;
- social content;
- analytics.

The Analyst supplies the factual and analytical foundation. The Analyst does not make final business decisions.

## Questions to answer

### Market
What is the local landscape of children's dance and related activities?

### Competition
Who are the meaningful local competitors and how do they position themselves?

### Audience
What are parents actually choosing, fearing, asking and comparing?

### Search
What does the local search landscape look like and which topic clusters appear promising?

### Content
Which questions are poorly answered online?
Which topics are overused and generic?
Where can the school's real expertise create a meaningful advantage?

### Local
What local events, organisations, communities and information sources matter?

### Opportunities
What should the future content engine pay attention to first?

## Required output
Produce:
1. `SERPUKHOV-MARKET-ANALYSIS.md`
2. `SERPUKHOV-AUDIENCE-ANALYSIS.md`
3. `SERPUKHOV-COMPETITOR-MAP.md`
4. `SERPUKHOV-SEARCH-AND-CONTENT-OPPORTUNITIES.md`
5. `SERPUKHOV-EXECUTIVE-BRIEF.md`
6. `SERPUKHOV-HANDOVER.md`

All documents must be saved in `02_RESEARCH/`.

## Research standard
Prefer primary/local sources where possible.
Record sources and dates.
Do not invent missing values.
When estimates are used, label them as estimates.

## First phase
Do not optimise for speed at the expense of research quality.
The first report becomes the baseline against which the Radar and later research processes will be designed.
