# SERPUKHOV — ANALYTICAL RESEARCH BRIEF

## Objective
Build a decision-grade knowledge base for a children's dance school and local content/SEO system in Serpukhov.

## Primary questions
1. What is the local market of children's dance and related activities?
2. Who are the main competitors?
3. What products, formats, prices and positioning are visible?
4. What are parents actually searching for?
5. What questions, fears and objections appear in public discussions?
6. What content do competitors publish and what remains underserved?
7. Which local topics can support useful SEO coverage?
8. Which local events, organizations and information sources matter?
9. What opportunities are realistic for a small expert school?
10. What data is still missing?

## Research principles
- Serpukhov only for the pilot.
- Prefer primary/local sources when available.
- Record source and date for material claims.
- Separate facts, inference and hypotheses.
- Do not treat search snippets as verified facts.
- Do not recommend actions merely because a competitor does them.

## Deliverables
1. Serpukhov Market Analysis
2. Competitor Map
3. Audience & Parent Questions Map
4. Search Demand / Semantic Opportunity Map
5. Content Opportunity Map
6. Local Information Source Map
7. Risks / Data Gaps
8. Executive Brief
9. Handover

## Final principle
The research is successful when another AI can use it without having to repeat the same discovery work.
