# ANALYST DELIVERABLE STANDARD

Every major research package must contain:

## 1. Executive Brief
A short management summary:
- key findings;
- important facts;
- opportunities;
- risks;
- unresolved questions;
- recommended next actions.

## 2. Main research
Detailed evidence and analysis.

## 3. Handover
State:
- what was completed;
- files created;
- confirmed findings;
- hypotheses;
- unresolved questions;
- data gaps;
- what the next AI can build from this;
- what should NOT yet be built.

## Document quality
Every major document should clearly distinguish:
FACT
SOURCE
ANALYSIS
CONCLUSION
RECOMMENDATION

Avoid unsupported certainty.
