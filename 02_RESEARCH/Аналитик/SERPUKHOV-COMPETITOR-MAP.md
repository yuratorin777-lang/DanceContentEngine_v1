# SERPUKHOV-COMPETITOR-MAP

**Research date:** 13 August 2026  
**Geography:** Serpukhov  
**Purpose:** Map meaningful children's dance competitors and adjacent alternatives

---

## 1. Competitive landscape summary

The local field can be divided into:

- **Sports/ballroom:** ЦТС ЦСКА / local sports-dance presence, Талант, Элеганс.
- **Modern dance:** Импульс, Marvel, SKYseven, Releve and other studios.
- **Classical/choreography:** Сильфида, Дебют and related cultural groups.
- **Cultural/ensemble:** Россияночка, Вертикаль, Клевер.
- **Multi-service children's centres:** STARlife and other development centres.

The market is therefore competitive both within dance and across adjacent extracurricular categories.

## 5. Important analytical observation

The strongest competitor differences are not simply "style A vs style B".

They include:

**training model**
- sport;
- stage;
- creative;
- developmental;
- cultural;

and

**proof model**
- medals;
- performances;
- visible child progress;
- teacher reputation;
- atmosphere;
- institutional recognition.

---

## 6. HYPOTHESIS

A parent may compare two providers from different dance segments when both satisfy the same underlying goal, for example:
- "give the child an active hobby";
- "help with confidence";
- "prepare for stage";
- "improve physical development";
- "keep the child meaningfully occupied."

This should be validated through direct parent interviews/surveys.

---

## 7. Competitor data gaps

Not reliably established for every competitor:
- current capacity;
- exact group sizes;
- retention;
- conversion from trial to paid;
- actual prices for every age group;
- parent acquisition channels;
- advertising spend;
- SEO traffic;
- revenue;
- staff turnover.

Do not infer these from public visibility.

---

## 8. Recommendation

For future content-gap analysis, benchmark competitors by **question and parent intent**, not only by domain/topic count. A competitor that publishes fewer pages may still dominate a specific high-intent local question.
