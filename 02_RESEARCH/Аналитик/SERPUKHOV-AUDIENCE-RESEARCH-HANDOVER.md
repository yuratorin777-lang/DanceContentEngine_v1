# 3. What is confirmed

### FACT
Parents are not the only actor: the child is the user whose experience affects continuation.

### FACT
Local parent reviews repeatedly mention child enjoyment, teacher fit, progress, convenience and performance. citeturn1search5turn1search6

### FACT
Russian extracurricular-choice research supports a broader decision model involving finances, infrastructure, information and parental criteria. citeturn3view0

### FACT
External youth-sport evidence supports a relationship between enjoyment/competence/social support/coach relationship and persistence, while dropout can also involve time, cost, competing priorities and pressure. citeturn2search0turn2search1turn2search7

### FACT
A local switching case exists: a family moved to Serpukhov and changed studios; the review reports a shift from needing to persuade the child to the child running to classes. One case does not establish a general switching mechanism. citeturn1search6

# 4. Interpretations

### INTERPRETATION
The most useful audience model is not «mother 30–40». It is a decision system segmented by child age, parent job, child-side need, involvement level and decision stage.

### INTERPRETATION
The decision is two-sided: parent validates value/trust/practicality; child validates fit through experience, enjoyment, competence and social environment.

### INTERPRETATION
The main information problem is uncertainty reduction, not simply lack of awareness that dance schools exist.

### INTERPRETATION
Adaptation is a diagnostic stage where the family must distinguish temporary difficulty from persistent mismatch. The evidence does not support a universal rule of either forcing or immediately stopping.

# 5. Hypotheses remaining

1. Preschool families may need substantially more adaptation/safety information than older-child families.
2. Older children may increase the weight of autonomy, peer environment, style relevance and schedule conflicts.
3. Switching families may need root-cause comparison rather than generic provider information.
4. Parent and child preferences may conflict at several decision stages.
5. «Ребёнку нравится» may be a necessary but insufficient condition for parent satisfaction; parents also seek evidence of development and quality.
6. Local parent decision criteria may differ by activity type (sport, modern, ballet/choreography, cultural ensemble).

No hypothesis above should be converted into a universal audience fact without first-party validation.

# 14. Final handover statement

**Audience & Decision Journey Research v2 is complete at the evidence level currently possible from owner knowledge + local public evidence + external research.**

The first baseline answered **what the market looks like**. This cycle adds **how the decision appears to work** and where the evidence stops.

The strongest usable conclusion is:

> The parent is a decision-maker, but the child is a co-determinant of retention. The family decision evolves through uncertainty reduction, fit testing, adaptation and repeated reassessment of value.

This is an analytical finding, not a final positioning or strategy.
