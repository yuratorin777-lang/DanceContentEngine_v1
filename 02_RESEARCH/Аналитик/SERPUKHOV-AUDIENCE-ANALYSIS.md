# SERPUKHOV-AUDIENCE-ANALYSIS

**Research date:** 13 August 2026  
**Geography:** Serpukhov, Moscow Region  
**Object:** Parents as decision-makers for children's extracurricular dance

---

## 1. Executive finding

Public evidence suggests that parents evaluate children's dance through a combination of:

- whether the child actually wants to attend;
- teacher's relationship with the child;
- visible progress;
- safety / age appropriateness;
- discipline and regularity;
- convenience;
- performance opportunities;
- atmosphere and peer group;
- communication with parents;
- price/value.

However, the current public evidence is largely qualitative and review-based. It is useful for identifying recurring decision themes, but it is NOT sufficient to estimate their relative importance statistically.

---

## 2. FACT: Child enjoyment is repeatedly mentioned

Local reviews of Dance Kids, Сильфида and other studios repeatedly describe children attending willingly, being excited about lessons and showing what they learned at home.

Examples:
- Dance Kids reviews mention children asking whether there will be dance class and attending willingly.
- A Сильфида review describes a child who previously needed persuasion but now runs to classes.
- Reviews of other dance studios similarly mention children enjoying classes.

Sources:
- https://yandex.com/maps/org/dance_kids/112848922775/reviews/ — accessed 13 Aug 2026.
- https://yandex.com/maps/org/silfida_studio/66467683028/reviews/ — accessed 13 Aug 2026.
- https://yandex.com/maps/org/studiya_sovremennogo_tantsa_iriny_zabubeninoy/117935614116/reviews/ — accessed 13 Aug 2026.

**INTERPRETATION:** Parent satisfaction is closely linked to the child's subjective experience, not only to technical outcomes.

---

## 3. FACT: Teacher-child relationship is a repeated theme

Reviews frequently praise:
- finding an approach to individual children;
- warmth;
- attention;
- ability to motivate;
- clear explanation;
- discipline without excessive harshness.

For example, reviews of Элеганс explicitly mention teachers finding an approach to children and parents; reviews of modern-dance studios also highlight attentive teachers and motivation.

Sources:
- https://uchistancevat.ru/serpuhov/tantsevalnaya-studiya-elegans-170852676021 — accessed 13 Aug 2026.
- https://yandex.com/maps/org/studiya_sovremennogo_tantsa_iriny_zabubeninoy/117935614116/reviews/ — accessed 13 Aug 2026.

**INTERPRETATION:** "Who teaches my child and how they interact with them" appears to be a major trust mechanism.

---

## 4. FACT: Parents notice visible progress

Reviews mention:
- better coordination;
- rhythm;
- technique;
- confidence;
- physical development;
- new skills;
- stage performance.

A Dance Kids review, for example, describes improvement in coordination and ability to follow rhythm after joining.

Source:
- https://yandex.com/maps/org/dance_kids/112848922775/related/ — accessed 13 Aug 2026.

**INTERPRETATION:** Parents need observable evidence that lessons produce change. "We teach dance" is weaker than demonstrating what changes over time.

---

## 5. FACT: Parents value organisational transparency

Examples found in reviews include:
- online payment;
- information in parent chats;
- visible classes / cameras;
- administrator responsiveness;
- clear communication;
- support around performances.

Sources:
- https://yandex.com/maps/org/dance_kids/112848922775/related/ — accessed 13 Aug 2026.
- https://yandex.by/maps/org/ovatsiya/122847511503/reviews/ — accessed 13 Aug 2026.
- https://uchistancevat.ru/serpuhov/tantsevalnaya-studiya-elegans-170852676021 — accessed 13 Aug 2026.

**INTERPRETATION:** The service is experienced by parents as an ongoing system, not just a weekly lesson.

---

## 6. FACT: Convenience appears in parent evidence

A Dance Kids review explicitly cites convenient location and online payment among selection advantages.

Source:
- https://yandex.com/maps/org/dance_kids/112848922775/related/ — accessed 13 Aug 2026.

**HYPOTHESIS:** For families with preschool children, location and schedule may strongly influence final selection. This should be validated with a larger parent sample.

---

## 7. FACT: Performance is an important visible outcome

Local providers repeatedly use:
- report concerts;
- competitions;
- public performances;
- costumes;
- stage numbers;
- festivals

as part of the experience.

Examples:
- Элеганс report concert at ДК "Россия".
- Россияночка open-air performance.
- Сильфида reviews mention competitions, numbers and costumes.

Sources:
- https://culture.ru/events/4484342/otchyotnyi-kontsert-elegans — accessed 13 Aug 2026.
- https://serp.mk.ru/culture/2026/06/07/rossiyanochka-stancevala-pod-otkrytym-nebom-u-dk-rossiya.html — accessed 13 Aug 2026.
- https://yandex.com/maps/org/silfida_studio/66467683028/reviews/ — accessed 13 Aug 2026.

**INTERPRETATION:** Performance functions both as a child motivation mechanism and as proof visible to parents.

---

## 8. FACT: Parents encounter several different value propositions

The local market communicates:
- health and physical development;
- confidence and self-expression;
- discipline;
- technical dance development;
- stage experience;
- competition;
- friendships;
- creativity;
- cultural participation.

Sources:
- provider websites and public listings cited throughout this package.

**INTERPRETATION:** Parent intent is heterogeneous. A single generic persona such as "мама 30–40 лет, хочет развития ребенка" is inadequate for content planning.

---

## 9. Audience decision framework

A useful analytical model is:

### Child-side factors
- "Мне нравится?"
- "Мне нравится педагог?"
- "Мне интересно?"
- "Я могу это делать?"
- "У меня получается?"

### Parent-side factors
- "Безопасно ли?"
- "Подходит ли возрасту?"
- "Есть ли результат?"
- "Какой педагог?"
- "Как устроена дисциплина?"
- "Сколько это стоит?"
- "Удобно ли ездить?"
- "Что будет через 3–12 месяцев?"
- "Будет ли ребёнок выступать?"
- "Как школа общается с родителями?"

These questions are partly evidence-based and partly analytical synthesis; they should be validated before being treated as a complete audience model.

---

## 10. HYPOTHESES requiring further validation

1. Parents of 3–5-year-olds need more reassurance about adaptation than parents of older children.
2. Parents of 6–9-year-olds increasingly compare progress, teachers and opportunities to perform.
3. Parents of older children may place greater weight on style relevance, social environment and level of training.
4. Families choosing sports ballroom may evaluate competition/progression differently from families choosing modern dance.
5. Convenience may become decisive when several comparable studios are available.

These are hypotheses, not established facts.

---

## 11. What is NOT established

Open sources do not allow reliable conclusions about:
- exact parent income segments;
- gender split of decision-makers;
- exact willingness to pay;
- most important criterion by percentage;
- churn reasons;
- actual objections at the point of sale;
- why families switch from one local studio to another at population scale.

---

## 12. Recommendation for research/content system

Future audience research should capture questions in the parents' own wording and tag each observation by:
**age × child goal × parent goal × fear/objection × decision stage × style × evidence source.**

This will make later content generation substantially more useful than a static demographic persona.
