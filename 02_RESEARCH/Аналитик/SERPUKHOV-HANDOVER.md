## 3. CONFIRMED FINDINGS

### Market
- Serpukhov has multiple children's dance providers.
- The market includes sports ballroom, modern dance, choreography/ballet and cultural ensembles.
- Preschool entry is common.
- Adjacent sports and developmental activities compete for the same family resources.
- Several providers are concentrated around recurring local activity hubs.

### Audience
Public reviews repeatedly mention:
- child enjoyment;
- teacher-child relationship;
- visible progress;
- atmosphere;
- communication;
- performance;
- convenience.

These are recurring signals, not population-level percentages.

### Competition
Important local examples include:
- Сильфида;
- Импульс;
- Releve;
- Элеганс;
- ЦТС ЦСКА / local sports-dance presence;
- STARlife;
- SKYseven;
- Marvel;
- Россияночка;
- municipal/cultural-house groups.

### Search
Observed search intent naturally divides into:
- category;
- age;
- style;
- choice/problem;
- logistics;
- local comparison.

Exact monthly search volumes were not established.

## 4. HYPOTHESES

The following should remain hypotheses until first-party validation:

1. Location and schedule are major selection factors.
2. Parent needs differ materially by child age.
3. Adaptation is especially important for younger children.
4. Parents of older children may compare style and training level more heavily.
5. Decision-support content may have greater strategic value than generic "benefits of dance" articles.

---

## 6. Source quality notes

### High confidence
- official provider pages;
- official municipal/cultural sources;
- official local publications when clearly sourced.

### Medium confidence
- reputable local directories;
- 2GIS/business listings;
- established local media.

### Signal only
- individual reviews;
- social posts;
- search-result snippets;
- user discussions.

A review is evidence that one person reported something; it is not evidence that all parents think the same way.

## 8. What should NOT yet be built

Do not treat this baseline as sufficient to create:

- a final school strategy;
- final positioning;
- a product portfolio;
- a pricing strategy;
- financial model;
- market-share forecast;
- definitive parent persona;
- definitive SEO keyword priorities with numeric volume;
- claims of competitor market leadership.

Those require additional evidence and/or explicit strategic work.
